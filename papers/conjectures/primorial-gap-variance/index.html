A Primorial Extremal Conjecture for Cyclic Reduced-Residue Gap Variance

Justin Du·14 November 2026

Abstract

For a squarefree integer with a fixed number of prime factors, consider the variance of its cyclic reduced-residue gaps. We conjecture that the corresponding primorial is the unique maximizer when there are at least two prime factors. The gap statistic is established; the extremal comparison is the subject of this note. We prove the conjecture for two and three factors, and for even squarefree integers with four factors. Exact short-gap distributions and monotonicity on reciprocal-prime parameter boxes give these infinite comparisons. We also derive a correlation formula for adjoining a sufficiently large prime and explain why clustered deletions obstruct its direct extension. Bounded exact checks accompany the proofs; the full conjecture remains open.

What is proved, and what remains open

The general primorial comparison remains open. The three-prime-factor theorem has no prime cutoff; the four-factor theorem covers only even squarefree integers.

Status & AI use

This is a proposed research problem with partial results, not a general proof or an externally peer-reviewed publication. Generative AI contributed substantially to exploration, derivations, manuscript text and verification code. The proofs received internal review and bounded exact checks; no independent human peer review or proof-assistant formalization is claimed.
