Research notes / 14 November 2026

Five proposed conjectures

Five AI-assisted proposals with partial theorems and exact checks. The general statements remain open. The maximum-ambiguity note poses an open extremal problem; the dyadic note formulates a conjecture within that problem.

A primorial extremum for cyclic reduced-residue gap variance. Complete proofs cover two and three prime factors, and the even four-factor class.

A One-Vertex-Deletion Conjecture for Square-Sum Hamiltonian Cycles

Can every square-sum graph of order at least 71 retain a Hamiltonian cycle after any one vertex is deleted? Exact graph estimates and local repair obstructions accompany finite certificates.

How many permutations can share one ordered adjacent-difference record? Growth-rate existence, endpoint bounds, and a completely solved infinite local family frame the open extremal problem.

A Dyadic Unique-Maximum Conjecture for Adjacent-Difference Fibers

A recursively defined record is conjectured to be uniquely optimal at every power of two. A spectral bound strengthens a restricted order-32 result; unrestricted order-32 optimality remains open.

A Path-Minimum Conjecture for the Variance of Uniform Independent-Set Size in Trees

Does the path minimize the variance of uniform independent-set size among trees? Local activity comparisons and an infinite branching-family theorem clarify the still-open activity-one question.

Proof review and evidence

The pre-publication review checked the new derivations, hypotheses, equality cases and unproved ranges. Exact polynomial identities and small-order tests supplement those arguments. The order-32 result remains restricted and depends on an archived 16-label exhaustive certificate. Historical large enumerations were not rerun. These are internally reviewed AI-assisted research drafts, not externally peer-reviewed results or an exhaustive priority certification.
