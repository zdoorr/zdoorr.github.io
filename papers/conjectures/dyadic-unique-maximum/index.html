A Dyadic Unique-Maximum Conjecture for Adjacent-Difference Fibers

Justin Du·14 November 2026

Abstract

We conjecture that a recursive ordered difference word is the unique largest permutation fiber at every order 2ᵏ. We give a complete hand-checkable four-label model, an exact endpoint-matrix recurrence, and rigidity under a prescribed dyadic distance multiset. At order 32 an archived exhaustive 16-label energy certificate proves uniqueness among all records with one odd entry. Within this class, a path-spectrum bridge bound improves the upper bound for competitors with a half different from D₁₆ to 47996, below the candidate's 48410. Parity-run balance and a two-odd-entry contraction identify the additional data needed beyond one crossing. The unrestricted order-32 inequality and the all-orders conjecture remain open.

What is proved, and what remains open

The 47,996 bound applies only to one-odd-entry order-32 records with at least one half different from D16. It depends on the archived exhaustive 16-label energy certificate. Global order-32 and higher-order optimality are not proved.

Status & AI use

This is a proposed research problem with partial results, not a general proof or an externally peer-reviewed publication. Generative AI contributed substantially to exploration, derivations, manuscript text and verification code. The proofs received internal review and bounded exact checks; no independent human peer review or proof-assistant formalization is claimed.
