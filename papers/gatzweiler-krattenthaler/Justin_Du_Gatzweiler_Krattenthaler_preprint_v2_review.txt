Justin Du | 11 November 2026

Status: internal AI-assisted review, supplemented by exact arithmetic checks.
This document is not external human peer review or proof-assistant formalization.

CHANGES

1. Lemma 5.1 now derives g not dividing c, and hence g > 1, from the
   statement that a and b are the only two multiples of g in B.
   The overall gcd normalization is unnecessary and has been removed.
2. The same lemma explicitly establishes
      2 <= |B intersect gZ| <= |A intersect gZ|
   before passing to the remaining two-multiple case.
3. The upper-endpoint lemma states why k >= 3l-1 implies d >= k/2 and
   expands the ceiling/floor inequality using Euclidean remainders.
4. Sumset lifts now use zeta, reserving z for a number of occupied cosets.
   The iterated-sumset estimate explicitly records t >= 2 and z >= 1.
5. PDF reproduction commands preserve literal ASCII double hyphens.
6. release_checks.json no longer lists external layout-reference inputs.
   Every distributed path/hash entry now refers to a file actually inside
   this archive. validate_package.py verifies this property mechanically.

SECTION-BY-SECTION REVIEW

Section 1: Gaussian-binomial symmetry and the strict increase of j(n-j)
on the reduced integer indices justify the normalization. Cyclotomic
multiplicities give precisely the stated divisibility condition.

Section 2: The set version of the regular-sequence criterion agrees with
Propositions 3.3 and 3.6 of the cited paper. The iterated Kneser inequality
is valid after quotienting by the final stabilizer: all intermediate
stabilizers in that quotient are trivial. The two-generator threshold
uses strict inequality and an explicit residue representative.

Section 3: The selected low generator belongs to I and does not exceed d.
The lift uses N > max(I), so no negative coefficient of that generator is
needed. Coset capacities count original generators rather than distinct
residues. The zero coset is counted once using the divisibility hypothesis.
The nonnegative difference in (3.9) handles all stabilizers.

Section 4: A collision pair's gcd is not assumed to divide the other
generators. Each occupied class uses its own translate c + <a,b>.
The exact capacity is the minimum of two tails with the same right end;
the max with zero handles an empty tail. Both component bounds are valid.

Section 5: The two-multiple case derives its conclusions from the actual
count in B. No overall coprimality assumption is needed. The four v-cases
and the neighboring-witness argument retain their stated interval bounds.
The new audit checks actual constructed witnesses, including nonprimitive
triples, against an independently computed semigroup.

Section 6: Reduction of A modulo m is used injectively only when d < m.
The zero-coset lower bound comes from the divisibility condition.
The v=1 branch uses r >= 4 and l <= 3(t-1); both hypotheses are available.

Section 7: Divisibility for k forces the unique possible multiple tk.
The proof that b lies in B uses d >= 2 and the interval's span d-1.
The resulting lower and upper bounds on N yield the claimed layer bounds.

Section 8: The boundary parameter restrictions and the excluded value of n
follow from the layer bounds and the missing multiple of 2l-1.
Both explicit maps have representable, distinct images inside A.

Section 9: d=1 is covered separately. For d>=2 the two general ranges and
the t=2 boundaries exhaust the parameters, including the l=0 cases.
The semigroup criterion is applied only after all subsets are covered.

RESULT

No additional substantive mathematical gap was identified in this review.
The conclusions are limited to this internal review and the exact finite
audits. The manuscript remains a candidate proof.
