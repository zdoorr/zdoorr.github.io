A candidate proof of the Gatzweiler–Krattenthaler positivity conjecture

Justin Du·11 November 2026

Abstract

Gatzweiler and Krattenthaler conjectured that a polynomial quotient of two Gaussian binomial coefficients with the same upper index has nonnegative coefficients. We present a candidate proof for the complete parameter range. After cancellation, polynomiality gives divisibility-count inequalities for two separated integer intervals of equal length. We prove that every subset of the lower interval generates at least as many elements of the upper interval in its additive semigroup. The criterion of Gatzweiler, Levicán-Santibáñez and Yoshida then supplies a regular sequence whose Hilbert series is the required quotient. The central lemma uses a mixed sumset modulo the largest generator not exceeding the interval length. Kneser’s theorem, together with separate capacities for the lower and upper generators, handles all stabilizers and all collisions of residues. Two-generator representations treat subsets near the upper endpoint. A second sumset estimate handles small lower indices, and a necessary inequality from polynomiality leaves only two explicit boundary families. The proof has no finite parameter cutoff; exact computations are supplied as supplementary checks.

Status & AI use

This is a candidate proof and has not undergone external human peer review or proof-assistant formalization. The manuscript was prepared in an AI-assisted workflow, including mathematical review and exposition, reference checking, verification reruns, and typesetting. The appendix distinguishes the symbolic proof from the supplementary finite checks.
